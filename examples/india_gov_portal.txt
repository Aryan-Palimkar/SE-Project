The higher education department of a state government wants to replace its paper-based scholarship process with a web portal and mobile app. Students will register with their Aadhaar-linked mobile number, upload marksheets and income certificates, and track their application; college officials will verify documents online, and the department will approve and pay scholarships through direct benefit transfer.

The portal will be hosted in the state data centre and must follow the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps, including accessibility for students with disabilities and content in English and the regional language. It must obtain a security audit clearance before going live. About three lakh students apply each year, almost all in a six-week window, so the portal must handle heavy peak load.

The scholarship rules are fixed by a government order for the academic year, and the department has fixed the budget and scope in a tender awarded to an IT firm. Department officials will be available for reviews at each milestone. The same department ran a smaller pilot portal last year, so the team knows the process, but the integration with the treasury payment system is new.
