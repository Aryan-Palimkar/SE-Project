Sahyadri Bank, a private sector bank regulated by the RBI, wants new savings account customers to complete KYC entirely through its mobile app using video-based customer identification. Today customers visit a branch with their Aadhaar and PAN cards, and staff upload the records to the Central KYC Records Registry the next day.

In the new flow the customer gives consent, completes Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC, uploads their PAN, and joins a live video call with a trained bank official who matches their face against the Aadhaar photo and asks a few questions. Calls from IP addresses outside India must be blocked, and the video recordings and all customer data must be stored on servers in India. Accounts must not become operational until the video check and a review of the recording are complete.

The app will hold Aadhaar numbers, PAN, photographs and video recordings, so the security team insists on encryption, access logs and a quick response to any breach. The bank's internal audit team will review the onboarding controls, and the RBI may inspect them. The account opening journey is new to the digital team, and the video-KYC vendor platform has not been integrated with the core banking system before. The bank expects about 5,000 applications a day and wants to launch before Diwali.
