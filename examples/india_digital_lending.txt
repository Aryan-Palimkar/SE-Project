An NBFC registered with the Reserve Bank of India is launching a mobile app for small personal loans of ₹10,000 to ₹2 lakh. Borrowers apply in the app, share bank statements through the Account Aggregator framework, and receive a decision within minutes. A lending service provider runs the app and the collection calls, while the NBFC makes the credit decision and disburses the loan directly to the borrower's bank account.

Before the borrower signs, the app must show a Key Fact Statement with the annual percentage rate, all fees and the cooling-off period, and no charge outside it may be levied later. The app may only access the phone's camera, microphone and location with the borrower's consent and must not read contacts or photos. Borrowers need a grievance officer they can contact from the app.

The credit team changes the scoring rules every month based on default rates, and the product team releases app updates every two weeks. The data includes PAN, Aadhaar-masked identity documents, income and bank transactions, and last year a competitor was hit by a data leak. The NBFC's compliance team must approve every rule change, and the first version should go live in three months.
